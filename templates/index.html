Key Verification Findings:

  • 0–30 min Horizon: High POD of 0.807 with ~50% reduction in False Alarm Ratio compared to persistence.
  • 60–120 min Horizon: Thermodynamic indices (CAPE and 0-6km Shear) maintain positive forecast skill (CSI 0.495 vs 0.217).
  • Limitation & False Alarms: Elevated false alarms occur when high Convective Inhibition (CIN > 100 J/kg) caps thunderstorm initiation despite elevated CAPE.
